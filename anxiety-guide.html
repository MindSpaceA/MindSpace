Understanding Anxiety

Understand what anxiety is, why it happens and explore healthy coping strategies for everyday life.

What is Anxiety?

Anxiety is a natural emotional response to stress, uncertainty, or challenging situations. It can help us prepare for important moments, but when anxiety becomes constant, it may affect our daily life, emotions, and overall wellbeing.

Understanding anxiety is the first step toward managing it in a healthier way.
