Understanding Anxiety

Understand what anxiety is, why it happens and explore healthy coping strategies for everyday life.

What is Anxiety?

Anxiety is a natural emotional response to stress, uncertainty, or challenging situations. It can help us prepare for important moments, but when anxiety becomes constant, it may affect our daily life, emotions, and overall wellbeing. Understanding anxiety is the first step toward managing it in a healthier way.

Common Signs of Anxiety

🌪️

Constant Worry

Feeling unable to stop worrying about situations, problems, or the future.

💭

Racing Thoughts

Having a busy mind and finding it difficult to relax or stay focused.

😰

Physical Symptoms

Feeling tension, restlessness, increased heartbeat, or discomfort.

🌙

Sleep Difficulties

Trouble falling asleep because of ongoing thoughts and worries.

Healthy Ways to Cope

🌬️

Deep Breathing

Slow breathing exercises can calm your body and reduce tension.

🧘

Stay Present

Mindfulness helps you focus on the current moment instead of worries.

🏃

Move Your Body

Regular movement supports a healthier mood and reduces stress.

💙

Share Your Feelings

Talking with trusted people can make difficult emotions easier to manage.

"You don't have to control every thought. Sometimes you just need to understand and accept them."

Find Your Inner Calm

Small steps and healthy habits can help you feel more balanced every day.
